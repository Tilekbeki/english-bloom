Словарь

bloom

/bluːm/

расцветать

These flowers bloom in spring.

recall

/rɪˈkɔːl/

вспоминать

Can you recall the answer?

review

/rɪˈvjuː/

A short break helps me focus.

/ˈhæbɪt/

improve

/ɪmˈpruːv/

I want to improve my English.
